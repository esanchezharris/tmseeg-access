What was checked

Event timing was checked on 479 of 479 TMS runs, and sidecars against headers on 479. Signal checks covered 40 runs from 10 subjects.

The signal-checked subjects were chosen to include known problems, so they are not a random sample. Do not read their rates as rates for the whole dataset.

Pulse spacing

Counting every gap of 2 ms or more, the median gap between markers is 3.023 s. Of 57,525 gaps, 13 fall in the published 4 to 6 s range. Gaps over 7 s are not drawn, but are in the data.

Disturbance around the pulse

20 of 40 signal-checked runs show a pulse artifact or a very large channel, and 13 peak at 1,000 uV or more. 13 of those record interpolation in their header, so the header alone cannot show a run is clean.

Peak channel is the largest deviation of any one channel's averaged response. Deviation is the median across channels, so it misses an artifact on a few channels. Field power counts only what differs between channels. All three are in the recorded reference.
