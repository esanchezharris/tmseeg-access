What was checked

Event timing was checked on 479 of 479 TMS runs, and sidecars against headers on 479. Signal checks covered 40 runs from 10 subjects.

The signal-checked subjects were chosen to include known problems, so they are not a random sample. Do not read their rates as rates for the whole dataset.

411 of 479 runs have the planned 120 markers. Counts near 240 are mostly markers recorded twice for one pulse.

Pulse spacing

The median gap between distinct pulses is 3.023 s. Of 57,525 gaps, 13 fall in the published 4 to 6 s range. Gaps over 7 s are not drawn, but are in the data.

Pulse artifact left in the signal

18 of 40 signal-checked runs keep a pulse artifact, and 8 peak at 1,000 uV or more. 8 of those record interpolation in their header, so the header alone cannot show a run is clean.

Deviation is measured on each channel in the recorded reference. Field power counts only what differs between channels, so a large deviation with a much smaller field power means most of the artifact is shared by every channel.
